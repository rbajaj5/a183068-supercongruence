# Unindexed research intake

This directory is the landing area for newly generated conjectures,
experiments, and proof sketches that have not passed the repository's
admission gates.

Material here is deliberately absent from `RESULT_INDEX.md` and `RANKINGS.md`.
Its presence is not evidence that a theorem is correct, new, important, or
ready for review.

An item may leave intake only after it has:

1. one exact quantified claim;
2. an immutable entry date and source classification;
3. separate author-completeness and audit statuses;
4. a proof or an explicit list of open gaps;
5. a checker with near-miss and range mutation results when computation is
   material;
6. the required dyadic or ramified-prime analysis; and
7. a named-corpus literature audit.

The active GWL-TWO gate is reviewed on 2026-08-16. Until that gate resolves,
new families remain here even if their internal arguments appear complete.
