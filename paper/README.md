# ArXiv review draft

`supercongruence-portfolio.tex` is the compact manuscript source prepared
for external review.  It is not an arXiv submission record and does not
claim peer review or literature priority.

The manuscript deliberately contains:

- complete proofs of the four flagship transfer mechanisms;
- a compact ledger of the named OEIS consequences currently classified as
  proved or as explicit classical deductions;
- a separate list of the most important reviewer obligations; and
- an explicit machine-assistance disclosure.

The compiled review copy is
[`../output/pdf/supercongruence-portfolio.pdf`](../output/pdf/supercongruence-portfolio.pdf).

Before an actual arXiv submission:

1. obtain independent specialist review;
2. confirm author order, affiliations, and contact information;
3. complete the priority audit for the family theorems;
4. decide whether the companion-proof ledger belongs in the paper or in a
   separate sequel; and
5. update the OEIS records only after the corresponding proofs are accepted
   by their editors or otherwise independently checked.
