# The extraspecial rank obstruction for $G_{\mathbb Q_2}$

## Result

Let $E$ be an extraspecial $2$-group of order

$$
|E|=2^{1+2n}.
$$

If $n\ge 2$, then

$$
\boxed{\left|\mathrm{Sur}(G_{\mathbb Q_2},E)\right|=0.}
$$

Thus no extraspecial group of order at least 32 occurs as the Galois
group of an extension of $\mathbb Q_2$.

The order 8 boundary is different. The two extraspecial groups there are
$D_8$ and $Q_8$, and both are quotients:

| Target | Surjections | Extension classes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $D_8$ | $144$ | $18$ |
| $Q_8$ | $144$ | $6$ |

The separate
[dihedral](GQ2DihedralCounts.md) and
[quaternion](GQ2QuaternionCounts.md) pages give those exact counts.

## Proof

Roe and Turturean identify the maximal pro-2 quotient of
$G_{\mathbb Q_2}$ with the three-generator group

$$
D_0=
\left\langle A,S,Y\ \middle|\ A^2S^4[S,Y]=1\right\rangle_{\mathrm{pro}\text{-}2}.
$$

Every homomorphism from $G_{\mathbb Q_2}$ to a finite $2$-group factors
through $D_0$. Consequently every finite $2$-group quotient of
$G_{\mathbb Q_2}$ can be generated by at most three elements.

For an extraspecial $2$-group,

$$
Z(E)=[E,E]=\Phi(E)\cong C_2
$$

and

$$
E/\Phi(E)\cong (C_2)^{2n}.
$$

The Burnside basis theorem therefore gives

$$
d(E)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2} E/\Phi(E)=2n,
$$

where $d(E)$ is the minimum number of generators of $E$. If $n\ge2$,
then $d(E)\ge4$, so a three-generated pro-2 group cannot surject onto
$E$. This proves the result.

## Why this changes the next-target map

The extraspecial family initially looked attractive because its central
commutator pairing is genuinely quadratic. The generator-rank check shows
that it cannot be tested by taking an extraspecial group itself as the
finite target.

A quadratic extraspecial layer could still occur inside a larger *framed
lifting problem*, where some generators are fixed by external data and the
extraspecial group is a kernel rather than the whole quotient. That is a
different problem. The pure finite-target count is already closed by the
rank obstruction above.

## Status and source boundary

This is an elementary consequence of the Roe--Turturean presentation and
the Burnside basis theorem. It is recorded to close a natural but
impossible target family and to prevent an unproductive computation. No
novelty claim is made.

The rank boundary is also asserted by
[`verify_gq2_maximal_cyclic_counts.py`](../verification/related/verify_gq2_maximal_cyclic_counts.py).
