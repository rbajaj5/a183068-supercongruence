# Research workflow and status gates

This document defines how a mathematical observation becomes a public result
in this repository. It exists to prevent a large research portfolio from
turning into an undifferentiated collection of claims.

## 1. One claim, one ledger entry

Every independently quotable statement receives an ID and an entry in
[`RESULT_INDEX.md`](RESULT_INDEX.md), even when several statements share one
proof note.

Each entry records:

- the exact quantified claim;
- proof status;
- source status;
- the \(p=2\) or ramified-prime burden, when relevant;
- the proof note;
- the checker, if one exists; and
- any known counterexample or excluded boundary.

## 2. Proof-status gates

```text
observation
    -> exact computation
    -> conjecture or reduction
    -> complete unchecked draft
    -> audited draft
    -> conventional specialist review
    -> submitted or published result
```

Finite computation can refute a claim or locate its boundary. It does not
promote a conjecture to a theorem.

### Observation

A pattern, analogy, or possible connection. It belongs in a notebook or
clearly marked synthesis section, not in the completed-results table.

### Computational

The statement has exact finite evidence with the domain, arithmetic model,
and search range recorded. The checker must be reproducible.

### Reduction

The original target has been proved equivalent to, or implied by, a smaller
explicit unresolved statement. Failed proof routes remain recorded.

### Complete unchecked draft

All logical steps are written. Exact checkers test transcription and boundary
cases. Literature priority and independent mathematical review remain open.

### Audited draft

A separate exact-text audit has attempted to rederive every displayed step,
checked small-prime boundaries, and recorded corrections. This is still not
peer review.

### Specialist-reviewed or published

Only conventional external review or a citable publication moves a result
past the audited-draft stage.

## 3. Source-status gates

Proof status and source status are independent.

- **Named open problem:** a cited source states substantially the same target.
- **Explicit source direction:** a source asks for this investigation but not
  the exact theorem.
- **Structural follow-on:** the target was generated by this program.
- **Known theorem or corollary:** the statement is already published or is an
  immediate specialization; novelty is not claimed.

A negative search never proves novelty. A priority note must list its
databases, structural queries, closest matches, and the remaining uncertainty.

## 4. Dyadic gate

Any theorem stated for every prime must pass
[`DYADIC_POLICY.md`](DYADIC_POLICY.md). At minimum it records:

1. valuation normalization at \(2\);
2. any scaling sign or unit ambiguity;
3. the first lift separately;
4. the cancellation or parity mechanism closing a lost power; and
5. exact boundary checks.

For Gaussian integers, the ramified prime \(1+i\) is a separate local case;
it is not obtained by substituting \(p=2\) into an odd-prime proof.

## 5. Verification gate

Checkers should:

- use exact integer, rational, finite-field, or Gaussian arithmetic;
- state every tested range;
- avoid floating-point tolerance unless the result is explicitly numerical;
- include boundary and known-failure cases;
- print a concise pass summary; and
- be registered in [`verification/run_all.py`](verification/run_all.py) when
  they are part of the maintained portfolio.

The repository-level integrity check validates local Markdown links, balanced
code/math fences, and the immutability of `BALA_VERSION.md`.

## 6. Presentation gate

Every public note begins with:

- a precise statement;
- a visible mathematical status;
- a source/priority boundary;
- a short explanation of what is genuinely improved; and
- a reproduction command when computation is material.

The root [`README.md`](README.md) is a dashboard. Detailed mathematical
arguments belong in dedicated notes.

## 7. Ranking gate

[`RANKINGS.md`](RANKINGS.md) scores:

- math-community value;
- deployment value;
- novelty confidence;
- breadth;
- maturity; and
- cost remaining.

These are resource-allocation estimates. They never override the controlling
status in [`RESULT_INDEX.md`](RESULT_INDEX.md).

## 8. Protected correspondence bridge

[`BALA_VERSION.md`](BALA_VERSION.md) is a stable public bridge to the
correspondence around A183068. Repository reorganizations may link to it but
must not rewrite, rename, or relocate it without an explicit separate
decision. Its content hash is enforced by the integrity checker.
